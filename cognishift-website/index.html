Signal Collection

Four signals. Zero effort.

Your brain leaks its state through micro-behaviors. CognitiveShift compares you only to yourself — never a population average.

⌨️

Inter-Keystroke Interval

Tracks timing variance between keypresses. ADHD brains show characteristic stutter-step bursts — rapid typing followed by 400ms+ lapses. Coefficient of variation, not raw speed.

🖱️

Cursor Jitter Score

Samples mouse at 60 Hz. Computes perpendicular deviation from the ideal path between two intentional clicks. Normal: 0.05–0.15. Fatigue threshold: above 0.30.

📜

Scroll Entropy

Direction reversals per unit of content. Calm reading scrolls linearly. Confused re-reading produces high-entropy back-and-forth without pausing at content boundaries.

👁️

Dwell / Re-read Ratio

IntersectionObserver tracks viewport dwell vs expected reading time. A ratio above 2.0 across multiple paragraphs is a strong cognitive load signal.
